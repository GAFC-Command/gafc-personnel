GAFC Personnel: Privacy Policy

Global Armed Forces Command (GAFC) is an online GTA roleplay Mil Sim community. This site is its private personnel system. It is for GAFC members only.

What we collect

  • From Discord sign-in: your Discord user ID, username, and the roles you hold in the GAFC Discord server.
  • What GAFC staff enter: your rank, billet, unit, counselings, award recommendations, and the history of actions taken on your record.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and decide what you can see based on your GAFC role.
  • To run the roster, counseling and award workflows, and to post updates to GAFC Discord channels.
  • To keep a personnel file and nightly backups for the group.

Google Drive

The GAFC Google account uses Google Drive only to store GAFC-owned files: approved counseling and award PDFs, and encrypted nightly backups. The app can see only the files it creates. It does not read other files in the account, and it does not access any member's personal Google data.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone outside GAFC leadership and the services that run this site (Supabase for the database and sign-in, GitHub for hosting, Discord for sign-in and messaging, Google Drive for file storage).

Retention and removal

Records stay in the personnel file while you are a member and afterward as GAFC leadership requires. To ask for a correction or removal, contact GAFC S1 or J1 in the Discord server.
